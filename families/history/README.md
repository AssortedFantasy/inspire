# History

Seventy-two short factual records about material practices, work, dress, and
technology. Six leaves each contain twelve records. Every record names its
period or historical source and carries a source URL in provenance.

Manuals describe their authors’ recommendations, not universal practice.
Museum models and garments are evidence about particular objects; their
interpretations stay scoped to those objects. Text is original paraphrase.
Public-domain manuals supply no medical, toxic-dye, or hazardous instructions.
