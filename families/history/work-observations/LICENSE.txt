Apollo 12 original mission transcript excerpts

Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), Apollo 12
Technical Air-to-Ground Voice Transcription, November 1969.
https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/as12-tec.pdf

The original NASA mission transcript is a work of the United States Government
and is in the public domain in the United States under 17 U.S.C. 105.
The Apollo 12 transcript archive explicitly distinguishes this original from
the later copyrighted corrected transcript:

"This is the raw transcript provided by NASA to the press during the mission.
No copyright is asserted. The corrected transcript which forms the foundation
of the Apollo 12 Lunar Surface Journal is covered by copyright."
https://apollojournals.org/alsj/a12/a12trans.html

This collection uses only the original transcript, not the corrected Lunar
Surface Journal or its commentary. NASA is credited with each excerpt.
Spoken words, false starts and source transcription quirks are retained.
Explicit scan-checked OCR corrections and source offsets are in selections.json.
