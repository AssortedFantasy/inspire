# Finite probability masses

Three equally sampled views retain ordinary PMFs and add comparisons with real
structural changes. Ordinary cases have 3–7 distinct integer outcomes in
[-9,12], weights 1–12, and exact mean, median set, and modes.

A mean-preserving spread splits one atom equally between two points 1–4 units
away, merging collisions with existing atoms. The pair prints the common mean
and both variances. The remaining view moves mass from the center of a symmetric
three-point distribution to its outer atoms. It samples adjacent cases at the
three-way mode tie, loss or appearance of support, and ordinary interior points.
Zero masses are omitted. All probabilities and moments use exact reduced fractions.

Tests verify moments against the pairwise variance identity, means and convex
call payoffs under spreads, and every supported mass-transfer parameter against
its variance, median, modal tie, and support cardinality.
