# Mathematics

`algebra` keeps the original polynomial identity generator. `integrals` creates
parameterized exercises with real domains and exact answers. `finite-groups`
samples seven explicit constructions with orders and computed products.

Three uniformly sampled corpora provide 24 definitions, 20 constructions, and
20 counterexamples spanning algebra, analysis, topology, and linear algebra.
Each record is newly worded and carries its reference context; examples include
enough structure or calculation to make the claimed distinction checkable.
References are sources for mathematical facts, not copied text. No source's
license is claimed for this original prose. Generator leaves document their
sources, bounds, assumptions, and invariant tests separately.
