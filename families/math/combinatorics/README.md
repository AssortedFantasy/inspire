# Combinatorics

`partitions` uses exact bounded enumeration, conjugation, Durfee decompositions
and odd/distinct-part bijections. `latin-squares` constructs noncyclic group
tables, mutually orthogonal field-linear pairs and intercalate trades.

These leaves sample construction regimes first. They do not claim uniform
sampling over integer partitions, Latin squares or isotopy classes. Their
READMEs give bounds, references and independently checked invariants.
