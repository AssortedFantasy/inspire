# Rings

Each leaf treats one type of mathematical material:

- `concepts/`: source-checked equations, maps, and concrete examples.
- `quadratic-fields/`: concrete quadratic extensions of finite prime fields,
  with separate views of arithmetic, inverses, Frobenius, trace/norm, and minimal
  polynomials.
- `residue-rings/`: composite integer quotient rings, including units, zero
  divisors, nilpotents, ideals, annihilators, CRT idempotents, and prime-power
  ideal chains.

The concepts corpus preserves stable record IDs from the former mixed
mathematics corpora. The two
procedural leaves keep field arithmetic distinct from composite-modulus ring
structure. Leaves have equal topic weights, independent of their record counts
or possible generated outcomes. Source and sampling details live beside each
payload. The conceptual records contain factual notation and calculations. Procedural
leaves use bounded exact constructions.
