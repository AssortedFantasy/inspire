# Commutative ring concepts

Original concise definitions and worked examples, with all nine pre-existing
record IDs preserved. Every sample names its ring and includes enough notation
to stand alone. This is a conceptual leaf; procedural ring families live beside it.

Primary reference pages consulted online on 2026-10-13:

- [Stacks Project conventions](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00AQ).
- [Basic notions](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00AR): ideals, quotients,
  idempotents, annihilators, local rings, and residue fields.
- [Localization](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00CM): multiplicative sets
  include 1, the fraction equivalence relation, kernel, and nilpotent localization.
- [Lemma 10.17.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00E0): radical ideals and
  their characterization as intersections of primes.

These are original formulations of mathematical facts with independently checked
examples, not a downloaded corpus or copied source prose. The Stacks Project
publishes its text under the GNU Free Documentation License; its prose is not
redistributed here. Provenance distinguishes reference context from calculations.
Records are sampled uniformly; IDs, text uniqueness, required fields, and absence
of NUL characters were checked after editing.
