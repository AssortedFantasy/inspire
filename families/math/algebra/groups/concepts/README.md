# Group theory concepts

Forty independent records cover group structure, homomorphisms, quotients,
actions, automorphisms, and finite-group theorems. Concrete small groups and
counterexamples keep definitions grounded. The original eleven records retain
their IDs and texts. This is one conceptual corpus; procedural constructions
belong in sibling leaves.

## Reference and reuse

J. S. Milne, [Group Theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf),
version 4.01, title-page date 6 November 2025, consulted 13 October 2026.
The PDF's revision history says 7 November 2025; the title-page date identifies
the version used here. Relevant sections actually inspected include kernels and
quotients, Theorem 1.45, cyclic-group automorphisms, Definition 3.8 and Example
3.9, group actions, Theorem 4.16, and Theorem 5.6. The alternating-group
simplicity statement is also listed in the finite simple-group overview.

The reference carries CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Its prose, exercises, and PDF are not
redistributed here. These records are original concise statements of standard
mathematical facts with independently worked examples, not an imported or
downloaded dataset. No suitable structured reusable dataset was used for this
bounded conceptual leaf; the reference was used to check theorem hypotheses.
