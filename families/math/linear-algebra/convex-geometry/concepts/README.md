# Convex geometry concepts

16 records of canonical formulas and concrete sets: convex hulls, intersections,
nonconvex unions, function spaces, affine maps, norm balls, halfspaces, polyhedra,
cones and hyperplanes. Records retain their IDs and source provenance.

The recorded references are Jiri Lebl's *Basic Analysis*, Section 8.1.4 and
related exercises, and Boyd and Vandenberghe's *Convex Optimization* slides,
Sections 2.2–2.10, 2.14 and 2.22–2.23. Per-record provenance retains the exact
source links and checks. Outputs contain factual notation and calculations,
rather than paraphrased source explanations.
