# Convex geometry concepts

16 self-contained records: the two original definitions plus fourteen facts,
examples, and counterexamples. Original IDs and text are preserved.

Sources read online on 2026-10-13:

- Jiri Lebl, [Basic Analysis, Section 8.1.4 and related exercises](https://www.jirka.org/ra/html/sec_vectorspaces.html):
  convexity, intersections, hull minimality, connected nonconvex sets, and
  convex sets of functions.
- Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization lecture slides](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxslides.pdf),
  Sections 2.2-2.10, 2.14, and 2.22-2.23: affine sets and maps, halfspaces,
  norm balls, polyhedra, cones, and separating/supporting hyperplanes.

These are independently written explanations of mathematical facts, not
downloaded source text or quotations. Numerical examples were checked directly;
new records identify the relevant source and their verification method in
provenance. Sources retain their own copyrights; no source prose is reproduced.
