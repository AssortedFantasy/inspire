# Vector spaces

18 original explanations and examples of standard mathematics. The two original
record IDs are preserved. Topics include field-dependent dimension, complements,
quotient spaces, rank-nullity, algebraic duality, and infinite-dimensional
counterexamples. This directory remains a corpus leaf.

Source checks performed 2026-10-13:

- Sheldon Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, fourth edition, author-hosted PDF,
  revision 2026-08-16: <https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf>.
  Chapters 1-3 supply the definitions and theorems cited in each record.
- Jiri Lebl, *Basic Analysis*, section 8.1, especially the basis discussion and
  proposition 8.1.17: <https://www.jirka.org/ra/html/sec_vectorspaces.html>.

These are factual references, not downloaded corpora or reproduced textbook
prose. Examples were written for this corpus and checked by their coordinates or
defining equations. Source-specific prose and exercises are not redistributed.
