# Texas hold'em showdowns

A shuffled deck supplies five board cards and two hole cards each for 2-6 players.
Every player is ranked by the best of all 21 five-card subsets of their seven
cards. Equal complete rank vectors split the result; suits never break ties.
Only the board, hole cards, categories, and winners are printed.

Tests cover shared board hands, wheel straights, full houses, and card conservation.
Betting and side pots are outside this final-card comparison.
