# Tic-tac-toe

Original positions generated by legal play: X starts, players alternate into empty
cells, and play stops immediately on a row/column/diagonal win or a full board.
The move history witnesses reachability. Random target lengths and uniformly
selected empty squares do not make positions uniformly distributed. Includes
ongoing games, wins for either player, and draws.

The exhaustive test enumerates the 5,478 reachable boards under the stated rules,
and checks sampled histories never continue after a win. The starting-player
convention is explicit rather than assumed to be universal.

Primary rule reference: [Hasbro's tic-tac-toe instructions](https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/dad261421c4311ddbd0b0800200c9a66/65D1A8C319B9F369105612AC9D65E71B.pdf)
for three-in-a-row wins and full-board draws. No published positions or wording
are copied.
