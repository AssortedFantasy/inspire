# Language

- `grammar/`: 50 original sentences with short explanations of English constructions.
- `idioms/`: 15 historical phrases with original definitions and usage examples.
- `proverbs/`: 25 sayings checked against Preston's numbered dictionary entries;
  the accompanying readings are interpretations, not universal advice.
- `word-histories/`: 12 concise explanations based on Weekley's 1922 edition.

Invented examples are explicitly identified in provenance. Idiom wording may be
inflected or modernized; attestation does not establish an expression's origin.
Historical sources are linked in each record. Grammar examples illustrate selected
constructions rather than claiming to describe every English dialect.
