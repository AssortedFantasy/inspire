# Voice and agreement

Five records cover subject–verb number agreement, existential `there`, agentful
and agentless passives, and the informal `get` passive. The syntax parent moved
the original apposition record intact to `../noun-phrases/records.json` to keep
this leaf coherent.

Every record was read for grammatical accuracy, example/explanation alignment,
self-contained output, and honest provenance. Examples and explanations are
original editorial material, not downloaded corpus observations or quotations.
No substantive correction was needed. The existential example explicitly limits
its agreement claim to formal usage; the passive examples identify an expressed
agent, an omitted agent, or an affected subject without universalizing those roles.

Validation: five nonempty records; exact `id`, `text`, `provenance` schema; unique
IDs and exact texts; no NUL characters. All original records were preserved.
