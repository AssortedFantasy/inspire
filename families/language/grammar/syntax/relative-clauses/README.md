# Relative clauses

Five original editorial examples cover restrictive, supplementary, zero-object,
possessive, and free relative clauses. These are invented illustrations of
conventional English, not downloaded corpus sentences or literary quotations.

All five records were reviewed in full. The restrictive clause identifies a
subset; the supplementary clause adds information about an identified referent;
the zero relative omits an object, not a subject; `whose` expresses possession;
and the free relative has no separate antecedent. Examples and explanations are
self-contained and coherent within this leaf. No corrections were needed; all
original records and IDs were retained.

Validation: exactly `id`, `text`, and `provenance` in each record; nonblank fields;
no NULs; five unique IDs and five unique texts.
