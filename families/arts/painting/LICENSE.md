# Cleveland Museum of Art metadata

The Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access catalogue is released under CC0 1.0:
https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Inspire selects 600 painting records from revision
d8dd744bc29e7986df428341133c14e18eb56b3f, with literal title, date, creators,
technique and culture. HTML and layout whitespace are cleaned. Only records
whose source share_license_status is CC0 are selected. No images, interpretive
essays or ownership histories are embedded. The associated drawing and
printmaking branches use the same source, selection and reuse terms.

Source CSV and exact SHA-256 are pinned in arts/import_paintings.py.
