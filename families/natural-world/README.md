# Natural world

Names, adaptations, and natural processes, kept in separate branches:

- `animals/`: mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, and invertebrates.
- `adaptations/`: anatomy, behavior, communication, and survival strategies.
- `phenomena/`: weather, geology, and ocean processes.

Animal-name leaf weights equal their record counts, so each name has the same
share within `animals`. Adaptation and phenomenon branches balance their topics
equally. General marine bioluminescence and counterillumination belong in ocean
phenomena because their descriptions span both vertebrates and invertebrates.

Every record has a source URL. Explanations are original paraphrases of government,
museum, or educational sources. Many animal names come from North American park
inventories; scientific names follow the cited source rather than implying a
comprehensive current taxonomy. Geographic and operating qualifications belong in
the stimulus whenever they affect the fact.
