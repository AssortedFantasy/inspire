# Natural world

Self-contained biological profiles, mechanisms, natural processes, and
astronomical bodies use separate branches:

- `animals/`: vertebrate and invertebrate taxonomy. Species summaries,
  genus-level syntheses, and source-specific observations use separate children
  when they coexist. Downloaded trait data add biological substance to names.
- `plants/`: vascular and nonvascular plants, with flowering plants separated
  by growth form and spore-bearing clades kept distinct. USDA profiles supply
  names, family, growth habit, lifecycle, and region-qualified native status.
- `adaptations/`: anatomy/physiology and behavior, subdivided by mechanism.
  Compact organism/trait fields replace explanatory prose.
- `phenomena/`: weather, geological internal/surface processes, and ocean
  circulation, tides, and ecology. Downloaded NOAA definitions broaden the
  concise process facts beyond clouds, hail, volcanoes, and karst. Glossary
  passages retain the National Weather Service's wording and credit.
- `astronomy/`: extrasolar discovery techniques and Solar System object kinds.
  Records retain discovery years, published estimates, projected minimum masses,
  radius definitions, uncertainties, and sidereal periods.

Natural-world topics receive equal conditional shares. Animal and plant
descendant weights equal record counts, making selection uniform per **record** within that
collection. Multiple animal observations of one species are separate records.
Extrasolar planets similarly give each retained object equal share. Other
branches balance mechanisms or object kinds.

Downloaded datasets have source snapshots, reproducible transformations, and
reuse terms beside their respective branches. Curated mechanism facts cite
their sources in each record. Raw inputs stay outside the sampling tree; the built
executable needs no source files or network. Factual estimates retain source
taxonomy, rank, regional, experimental, and measurement context where it affects
meaning. Attribution accompanies emitted data where required, and source-license
notices are available offline through the CLI.
