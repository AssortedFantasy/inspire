Inspire joins exact scientific names, selects available species estimates,
omits missing values and EXT columns, and rounds to three significant figures.
No imputation or synonym mapping is performed. Values are from the 2008
species concept and may include source model adjustments; maximum recorded
longevity includes captive and wild observations, and is not life expectancy.
