# Insects

125 records in two evidence-specific leaves:

- `species`: 66 named species spanning 19 source orders and 45 families,
  including all 25 original butterfly taxa. Downloaded GBIF nomenclature and
  classification supplement retained NPS inventory names.
- `observations`: 59 study-linked measurements across 28 source-listed families
  from AnimalTraits v1.0.7. Values describe sampled individuals or groups.

Classification follows pinned sources and can be historical. Species records
do not infer range, conservation status, or behavior; observations do not assert
universal species properties. Each leaf documents its selection, exact inputs,
replay procedure, and source limitations. Full GBIF CC BY 4.0 and AnimalTraits
CC0 notices are in [the animal branch](../../LICENSE.txt).

Both actual leaf agents reviewed their outputs, verified byte-identical offline
replay, and checked rejection of corrupted input before payload changes.
`review_manifest.json` records their coverage and substantive checks. Local
weights equal descendant counts: 66, 59, and branch weight 125.
