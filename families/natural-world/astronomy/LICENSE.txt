Astronomical numerical-data acknowledgements

These datasets format factual astronomical parameters from NASA Exoplanet Archive
and NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics. No article prose, images, or logos are copied.
The numerical facts retain their source identities and bibliographic references;
no blanket CC0 or public-domain dedication is asserted for third-party research.

The NASA Exoplanet Archive is operated by the California Institute of Technology
under contract with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration's Exoplanet
Exploration Program. Inspire acknowledges its services and the individual default
literature references attached to the Planetary Systems parameters.
Acknowledgement guidance:
https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/acknowledge.html
Archive publication: Christiansen et al. (2025), doi:10.3847/PSJ/ade3c2.

NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics physical parameter tables:
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/phys_par/

Original author/reference markers and quoted satellite-radius uncertainties are
retained in the pinned source inputs. Each record identifies its object and source
snapshot; formatted outputs are Inspire's curated numerical summaries.
