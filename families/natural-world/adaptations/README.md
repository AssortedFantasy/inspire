# Adaptations

Organism traits and behaviors are grouped by mechanism. Each record contains
concise factual fields drawn from its cited Smithsonian or National Park Service
source. The collection covers anatomy, feeding, locomotion, protection,
communication, habitat use, and social organization.

These are curated facts rather than rewritten source passages. Regional scope
is explicit for Yellowstone observations. Source URLs and consultation dates
remain in provenance. Mechanism branches have equal local weights.
