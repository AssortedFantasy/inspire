# Carbohydrates

Selected fixed chemical identities from pinned PubChem PUG REST factual and
computed property data. Monosaccharides, oligosaccharides and their derivatives
are reviewed separately; indefinite polymers are excluded.

PubChem structures may be cyclic or open-chain and may specify only some
stereocentres. D/L describes configuration, not optical rotation. A structure
with an unspecified anomeric centre does not identify a single anomer; a
specified cyclic form does not describe every form present in solution.

The six original CIDs (5793, 2723872, 5988, 6134, 439186, 10975657) are retained.
Subtree importers download only during development and use cached SHA-256-pinned
responses under `target/chemistry-sources/`. Runtime remains offline.

Reuse scope is factual identity, numerical properties and computed structures,
with original formatting and chemical classification. No third-party descriptive
annotations, figures or safety prose are included. Each record credits
PubChem/NCBI/NLM. NCBI's policy distinguishes government material from potentially
protected contributed material; no blanket public-domain claim is made.
See [chemistry data notices](../../../../LICENSE.md) and the
[primary NCBI policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/).
