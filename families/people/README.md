# People

Curated factual names with compact descriptions of a field or contribution.
The categories are browsing aids; people often worked across several fields.
The selection is not a ranking, comprehensive history or endorsement.
Local weights follow descendant record counts, giving every person the same
share when sampling `people`, regardless of folder depth.

Each record identifies the reference used for its original description.
Science and literature use Nobel Prize Outreach historical accounts and award
citations; mathematics uses the University of St Andrews MacTutor biographies.
Natural history uses museum collections and National Park Service accounts;
invention uses National Inventors Hall of Fame biographies. Only compact factual
descriptions are included, not copied biographies, quotations or images.

Names span several continents and eras, but the selection remains partial and
reflects the coverage of these sources. IDs describe the person and remain stable
if the record moves between fields.
