# Civil dates

The three leaves describe different representations of a whole calendar date:
Gregorian month/day, ISO week-year/week/weekday, and Gregorian day-of-year.
Each samples uniformly over the 219,146 dates from 1600-01-01 through
2199-12-31. Each has local weight 1.

ISO week and ordinal outputs include the equivalent Gregorian date, making the
representation directly interpretable. These are proleptic Gregorian dates;
they do not imply a local historical calendar or timezone.

See [the dates branch](../README.md) for calendar conventions and sources.
