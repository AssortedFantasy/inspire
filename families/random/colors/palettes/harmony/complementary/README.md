# Complementary palette

Five swatches share two HSL hues exactly 180° apart. Base and accent have
45–75% saturation and 42–56% lightness; their tints share 80–90% lightness
and 15 percentage points less saturation. The base shade has 18–28% lightness
and 10 percentage points less saturation.

Seeded tests check hue relationships, RGB swatch uniqueness, brightness order,
parameter coverage and reproducibility. No text contrast threshold is implied.
