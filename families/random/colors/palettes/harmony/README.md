# Hue harmonies

`analogous` emits five hues, at equal saturation and lightness, spanning a
40–80° arc in HSL. `complementary` emits a base, its tint and shade, an opposite
accent, and the accent tint. Each family preserves an explicit relationship;
HSL relationships do not imply perceptual uniformity, text contrast, or
color vision accessibility.

Both children have equal local weight. Hues are uniform integer degrees;
bounded saturation and lightness avoid nearly gray or nearly black accidents.
Syntax follows [CSS Color 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#the-hsl-notation).
