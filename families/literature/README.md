# Literature

- `quotations/`: 10 quotations checked against the cited primary works.
- `prose/`: 10 brief excerpts from six nineteenth-century authors.
- `dialogue/`: 6 exchanges from Austen, Carroll, Dickens, and Conan Doyle.
- `verse/`: opening quatrains from 10 Shakespeare sonnets.
- `poetic-forms/`: 12 original explanatory notes on conventional verse structures.

Literary text comes from public-domain English works published in 1609–1892.
Every excerpt identifies its author, work, and location in the printed stimulus;
provenance links the checked Gutenberg transcription. Page-number artifacts and
decorative drop-cap spacing are removed. An explicit `[…]` marks an omission
inside a quotation. Character speech is attributed as fiction.

Form notes paraphrase facts from the Academy of American Poets and Poetry
Foundation glossaries; they do not reproduce those sites' example poems.
