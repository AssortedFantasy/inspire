# Blazons

Original armorial combinations with a blazon and a literal description. Coverage
includes single ordinaries with plain or complex edges, single and three-charge
groups, charges on ordinaries, counterchanging across a two-part field, same-class
divided fields under a contrasting charge, fur fields, and uncharged paly/barry
fields. Plain descriptions use the viewer's left and right. Mullets have five
points, crescents point upward, lions are rampant, and eagles are displayed.

The contrast model follows the cited SCA usage: solid overlays require opposite
metal/colour classes; a tertiary charge contrasts with its ordinary. Counterchange
exchanges one metal and one colour locally. Ermine has a metal background,
counter-ermine a colour background, and vair excludes charges matching its silver
or blue constituents. Tests check each layer and both sides of a counterchange.

This is a scoped blazon grammar, not a registerability or novelty checker. It does
not check protected arms, marshalling, heraldic period, jurisdictional exceptions,
or actual rendering. Furs are not treated as universally neutral. Canting arms
and unrestricted multi-charge compositions are outside the model.
