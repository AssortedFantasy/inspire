# Folded-sheet imposition

Original 2-up plans for single-fold folios nested as booklets or sewn in multiple
gatherings. Architectures include equal sections and a shorter terminal section,
left or right binding, continuation page numbers, and zero to three terminal
blanks. A sheet carries two upright pages on each face. Both faces are shown
as viewed directly, rather than as an X-ray through the paper; turning a sheet
over a vertical axis aligns front-left with back-right.

The physical test folds sheets into front/back leaf pairs and nests those leaves
from outside to inside. Reading the resulting leaf stack must recover the full
consecutive page sequence in either binding direction. A separate check proves
that each page has exactly one sheet position and all blanks are at the end.

Scope is deliberately single-fold 2-up: no cross-fold quarto/octavo, duodecimo
offcuts, work-and-turn press forms, creep, bleed, or printer duplex-driver
settings. Sheet dimensions are untrimmed double-page rectangles. Gathering order
is the binding order. This restriction gives a proved fold sequence instead of
assuming that a page permutation will fold correctly.

Primary convention reference, checked 2026-10-22:
[Adobe InDesign booklet types](https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/print/print-booklets/booklet-types.html).
The imposition and physical reconstruction algorithms are original.
