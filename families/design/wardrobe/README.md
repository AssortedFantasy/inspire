# Wardrobe

Invented outfit suggestions for errands, a business-casual office, a smart-casual
dinner, or a maintained-trail day hike. A curated palette assigns light and dark
neutrals, one accent, and compatible footwear. Garments and layers follow warm,
mild, rainy, or cold weather; rain gets waterproof outerwear and suitable shoes,
and cold gets insulation, full-length bottoms, and gloves.

This is a compact design prompt, not personal styling, dress-code guidance, or
equipment advice for extreme weather or technical hiking. Temperatures are
scenario context, not a guarantee of comfort. No body or gender assumptions.
