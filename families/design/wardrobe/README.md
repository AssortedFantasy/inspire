# Wardrobe

Invented outfits for errands, a business-casual office, a smart-casual
dinner, or a maintained-trail day hike. A curated palette assigns light and dark
neutrals, one accent, and compatible footwear. Garments and layers follow warm,
mild, rainy, or cold weather; rain gets waterproof outerwear and suitable shoes,
and cold gets insulation, full-length bottoms, and gloves.

Each output specifies the garments and an actual accent item. Temperatures are
scenario context, not a guarantee of comfort or equipment suitability for extreme
weather or technical hiking. No body or gender assumptions.
