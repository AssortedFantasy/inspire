# Examples

Inspire gives a model a random starting point for a creative task. Below: five
outputs with Inspire and five without, using the same prompt.

## Short poems

> Write a short poem of 4–8 lines.

| The moon collects its silver change<br>From puddles in the street;<br>A moth, mistaken for a star,<br>Makes night feel incomplete. | In a narrow river of ink,<br>an elephant gathers its weight.<br>Three centuries pass over the paper;<br>still its raised foot does not fall.<br>Somewhere beyond the border,<br>the grass is waiting to bend. |
| The evening folds its paper wings<br>Above the quiet town,<br>And one by one, the windows bloom<br>As darkness settles down.<br>A moth taps softly at the glass,<br>A star that wandered close. | Three locks held fast against the night,<br>each with its own small chance to fail.<br>Only together could they yield<br>and let the darkness through.<br><br>By morning, all three keys had turned;<br>a single star fell down the stair. |
| The morning spills across the floor,<br>A quiet gold beneath the door.<br>The world outside begins to hum;<br>I let the light tell me to come. | In the quiet grammar of the garden,<br>asparagus lifts its green-tipped spears.<br>Morning weighs each stalk in dew,<br>and spring needs no more than that. |
| The morning folds its silver wings<br>And settles softly on the street.<br>A window wakes, a kettle sings,<br>The day begins with quiet feet.<br>Somewhere, beneath the thawing ground,<br>A seed turns toward the light it found. | We named the wind to warn the wheat,<br>the dark cloud to bring the washing in.<br>All afternoon the words flew overhead.<br>At dusk I folded the warm, dry sheets<br>and carried summer up the stairs. |
| Morning unbuttons the dark,<br>and rain slips off the leaves.<br>A robin tests one note,<br>then sings as if it believes. | Some stars are wounds in the dark.<br>Some wounds are windows.<br>So I mistook the night for a house<br>and every light for someone waiting.<br><br>Still, I stood outside until dawn. |
